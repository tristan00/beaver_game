# Beaver Colony — Style Guide

**One sentence:** a warm picture-book meadow seen from above, made of soft rounded shapes, lit by gentle morning sun, where every beaver looks like a plush toy and every panel looks like a cream-coloured cookie.

Audience: people who want a cosy, low-pressure colony toy (think calm, curious, pet-sized). Nothing is sharp, black, grey-blue or dark. When unsure, make it rounder, lighter, warmer.

Targets: `docs/style_mockup.png` (map-first screen) and `docs/style_icons.png` (icon sheet). Both are generated by `tools/make_style_art.py` from the values below. Regenerate, do not repaint.

## 3. Shape language and proportions

- **Everything is a blob.** Hills are overlapping ellipses; canopy is 3–4 overlapping circles; the lodge is a dome. No hard polygon silhouettes except the stone ridge, and its corners are bevelled.
- Beaver is **chibi**: head ≈ 60% of body width, tail a flat oval, eyes small black dots (`#4D3321`).
- Scale: a beaver is about 1 grid cell wide; tree canopy 2× a beaver; lodge 4× a beaver. Never realistic scale.
- UI corner radii: cards 22 px, buttons and pills full-round (height/2), tooltips 16 px. Padding 14–16 px. Nothing square on screen.
- Outlines: none on terrain; 2 px darker-fur outline only on small wooden pieces so they read at distance.

## 4. Materials, ground detail and water

- **Grass:** flat-shaded soft gradient from Meadow light to Meadow deep, with large lighter patches (ellipses, 60–140 px) for variety. No brush noise.
- **Ground detail:** sparse and small: grass tufts (two 8 px strokes, Lily/Meadow deep), flowers (5 px dots `#FFFAEE`, `#F59AA6`, `#F2C94C`), pebbles (10×6 px ovals `#B9AFA8`). About 1 item per 40×40 px of open grass, never on water.
- **Water:** soft two-ring pond: Deep rim, Shallow body, Mid glow centre; Foam ring 5 px at the shore; short Foam ripple arcs (30×9 px); lily pads. Edge is smooth, not a hard polygon.
- **Wood:** matte, rounded ends with visible cut rings.

## 5. Lighting and mood

Late-morning sun from the upper left, warm (`#FFF5E0`), soft low-contrast shadows in plum at 35–45%. Ambient is high so nothing is ever black. A faint warm glow hangs in the upper-left corner. Seasons shift hue only: spring fresh green, summer saturated, autumn Straw, winter pale lilac sky `#C8D4E8`; brightness stays high. Night is dusk blue-lilac `#6B5A8A` overlay at 30%, not dark.

## 6. UI style

Cream cards with a soft plum drop shadow (blur 9, y+3, `#6B5A8A` at 28%), floating over the map; the map is the hero, UI covers under 25% of the screen. Text in Ink on cream; two weights only (bold titles, regular body); sentence case; minimum 12 px. Progress bars: Track background, Good leaf fill, round ends. Buttons: pills, Button green when primary, Chip when secondary, icon left and label right.

**Navigation bar:** one floating pill bottom-centre (`docs/style_mockup.png`): four map-mode buttons (Water, Forest, Food, Work) then a divider then three action buttons (Build, Assign, Move area). Active mode is Button green; others Chip. Resources and needs sit in a top strip pill; speed in a small pill top-right; date in a pill top-left.

## 8. Fixes for the current screenshots

Each fix names the screenshot that shows the problem. Screenshots: **A** `docs/midgame.png`, **B** `artifacts/R12-14/dam.png`, **C** `docs/lodge.png`, **D** `artifacts/R12-9/top.png`. Hex values in "now" were sampled from those files.

**Terrain shading**
1. Rock and cliffs are cold blue-grey (A at 300,600 is `#494F57`; at 1200,200 `#A5B0BF`; C at 100,200 `#797E81`). Recolour all rock to Stone `#B9AFA8` lit and Stone shade `#8C8290` shadowed.
2. Valleys go near-black (B at 500,650 `#173424`; C at 300,150 `#454A50`; D at 640,520 `#303C39`). Clamp the darkest terrain value to Meadow deep `#5FA85A` or Stone shade; raise ambient so nothing is under `#5FA85A`/`#8C8290`.
3. Grass is flat saturated lime with smeared brush noise (D at 400,200 `#8AC863`). Replace with the Meadow light to Meadow deep gradient plus large soft patches (§4).
4. Slopes show hard, stepped facets (A, centre; C, top-left). Smooth the vertex normals so hills read as rounded.

**Ground detail**
5. Open ground is empty (A, D). Scatter tufts, flowers and pebbles per §4.
6. Foreground conifers are dark and spiky (B at 1000,200 `#2B3327`; C top-left). Replace with the round three-to-four-blob canopy, Meadow deep `#5FA85A` base with Leaf highlight `#8CD070`.

**Water**
7. Pond is a flat blue translucent square with a hard edge (B at 560,330 `#9DB6A6`). Use the rounded Deep, Shallow, Mid glow rings with a Foam shore ring.
8. Lowland water reads grey and muddy (D at 800,600 `#87AFAE`). Use Shallow `#6FD3E0` over Deep `#1F8AB8` and no grey.
9. The wet rings around swimming beavers are plain white discs (C, bottom). Use Foam `#F4FBF6` ripple arcs instead.

**Lighting**
10. Sky and light are pure harsh blue with black-ish shadows (D at 100,100 `#8AC5F5`; C at 200,640 `#314D5C`). Sky `#9ED1F5`; shadows plum `#6B5A8A` at 40%; upper-left warm glow.
11. The overall picture is dim and low-contrast under panels (A lower half, at 300,600 `#494F57`). Raise ambient so the average map brightness is above mid-grey.

**UI cards**
12. Cards (A left; B left) are already cream but are four tall stacked ledger cards covering the left third, one of them the fourth "offers" card that is outside the target screen. Keep a single Jobs card, `PANEL` fill, radius 22; move resources and needs to the top strip.
13. Event cards use thin colour bars and green row buttons stretching full width (A: colour bar at 614,60 `#93B78A`, row button at 700,180 `#9ECC8C`). Use the pill buttons with the Accept and Turn away icons, as in the mockup.

**Navigation bar**
14. The top bar mixes text and slate buttons (A at 1070,22 `#394C60`; B at 1180,22 `#333B47`; C at 1080,22 `#394D62`). Remove slate. Replace with the bottom-centre pill (§6) and the small speed pill top-right in Chip and Button green.

**Panels**

**Tooltips**
