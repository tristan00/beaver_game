METHOD
Release-mode headless runner linked to the repository's actual sim crate. Telemark,
seed from its map definition; normal founding location chosen by settle best.
The attentive scripted player used regular commands, alert fixes, construction
materials, worker labor, research, fog, stores, births and immigration. Additional
normal commands exercised manual controls. No resource grants, instant builds,
instant excavation, forced unlocks, or sandbox flags were used in this colony.

The run executed 1,036,800 one-minute colony ticks. It used the live
adaptive SimClock logic at X3, sampled every 20 ticks with the corresponding target
frame duration. The copied controller differed only in visibility and removal of
the Bevy Resource annotation. The game simulation source was unchanged. The
hydraulic clock stayed near its 60-second/day floor for most of the run.
This is not a rendered FPS, mouse/keyboard UX, or visual layering test.

Default scoped feature flags were kept: ordinary weather, seasons and colony
systems ran; disabled hazard/event systems (predators, special flood/drought events,
hard freeze, fire, disease, rivals, trade, guard and the abstract scouting job)
were not enabled. Manual exploration through move orders was exercised.

Confirmed findings in build 54e7bc4

P1 - Housing maintenance cannot keep pace with a larger colony.
All six kits died from Cold around day 694.655 despite assigned homes and over
1500 food. At day 690 their warmth was about 0.103 and health about 0.105; they
were fed and fully rested. This is a serious winter survival failure.
The only real-agent repair path is passive_repair: WORK_FRACTION (0.6) times
REPAIR_YIELD (0.04), capped at 0.024 condition/day for the ENTIRE colony.
Each home wears by up to 0.006/day in winter. Five wet homes therefore exceed
the budget; four use it all with no recovery margin. Each high-and-dry home
adds another 0.03/day, individually exceeding the whole repair budget. Adding
builders does not increase this repair path because derived-job do_work skips
abstract builder repair. Timber was available: 14.3 logs on day 675 and 12.9
on day 690, above the repair minimum of 2. This fixed repair capacity, together
with dry-home wear and the water-clock regression, needs correction and another
winter test. Home condition was not directly included in snapshots; poor thermal
protection is observed through needs, and the repair-capacity mismatch is verified
from code. Do not attribute every injury-labeled adult death solely to housing.
Relevant code: crates/sim/src/colony.rs (do_work, passive_repair, wear, update_needs);
crates/sim/src/lib.rs (wear_dry_homes); crates/sim/src/tuning.rs.

P1 - Adaptive water pacing changes the amount of water available to the colony.
The controller repeatedly reached its 60 hydraulic seconds/game-day floor. Catchment
inflow uses that shortened hydraulic duration, while precipitation, evaporation and
seepage use the full calendar duration. Identical clear-weather, five-day diagnostic
runs supplied 1.2267 m3 at 60 s/day and 1766.4568 m3 at 86400 s/day: a 1440-fold
difference caused by the clock setting. Final water volumes were 58.8087 and
109.7701 m3 from the same 159.7185 m3 starting state. These were separate water-only
diagnostics, not substituted for the colony playtest.
In the colony, water-dependent buildings became high and dry before the first dam
was built. Staffing Workshop #12 was refused on day 113 for lack of water. Food
production repeatedly stopped in summer; rain restored access. Map water reached
2.64565 m3 on day 158. The pacing fix therefore changes gameplay with machine load.
Preserve water delivered/lost per calendar day while optimizing integration work.
Relevant code: crates/game/src/sim_driver.rs (record_water_cost);
crates/sim/src/water.rs (step_with_clock, sourced-volume accounting).

P1 - Full-colony simulation misses the fastest calendar target.
The measured simulation-core time already exceeds the 1.25 real seconds/game-day
target, without rendering or scripted-player overhead. See the full-run average
and phase costs above. Water and agent updates dominate. The adaptive
water controller reaches its lower limit and cannot maintain the promised pace.
This is a headless elapsed-time measurement on the shared machine, not an FPS test.
Relevant code: crates/sim/src/lib.rs (step_with_clock); crates/sim/src/agents.rs;
crates/game/src/sim_driver.rs (fixed minute ticks and adaptive controller).

P1 - Valid building placements can seal a lodge entrance.
Lodge #9 at (174,382) has its entrance at (175,385). Completed ForesterLodge #22 at
(174,385) covers that entrance. Several adult Rest agents repeatedly failed to
enter homes while agent-update costs grew. An isolated placement/navigation
reproduction accepted both buildings, made the entrance Solid, and found no path
inside. Moving the forester aside restored a path. That fixture was separate from
the normal colony run. Placement checks reserve footprints but not the lodge's
extra entrance cell. Reserve entrances and check reachability when placing sites;
avoid retrying a known impossible home path on every minute tick.
Relevant code: crates/sim/src/nav.rs (lodge_entrance);
crates/sim/src/buildings.rs (check_ground); crates/sim/src/agents.rs (Task::Rest).

P2 - Resting burrow residents are falsely counted as stuck.
The burrow-resident kits #5/#6/#8 stood beside their burrow, Task::Rest, path=None,
inside=false, while stuck_ticks grew from about 18221 at day 60 to hundreds of
thousands later. route_home correctly routes burrow residents beside the burrow,
but Rest recognizes arrival only through inside=true. A zero-length path then
increments the stuck counter forever. Treat reaching the burrow-side resting
position as success. This is separate from the adult blocked-lodge problem; no
production loss was established from these kit counters alone.
Relevant code: crates/sim/src/agents.rs (route_home, note, Task::Rest).

P2 - Beaver ages drift from the calendar.
age_days is f32 and receives a tiny increment every simulated minute. Founder Iris
started at 1080 days old but was 1269.8438 after 180 calendar days, rather than
1260. A direct f32 replay gives a 39.375-day excess after 720 days. Iris died
before the end; his observed day-690 error was +37.7344 days. This affects
maturation and aging.
Use a higher-precision age clock or derive age from a birth timestamp.
Relevant code: crates/sim/src/colony.rs (age_days increment).


BALANCE OBSERVATION (NOT COUNTED AS AN ADDITIONAL CONFIRMED DEFECT)
Winter food production stayed strong: reserves rose from 1239.6 on day 630 to
1570.2 on day 660. Wet plants regrow at a constant 0.5 units/day in
resources::ResourceWorld::step, and wetland sprouting has no season/temperature
check. The temperature-based forage multiplier in colony::do_work applies to
abstract gathering, which this real-agent colony does not use. Foragers can also
collect bark. Review seasonal food availability if winter scarcity is intended;
the observations alone do not establish the intended balance.

LIMITS
One map, one attentive policy with recorded interventions, one complete two-year
run. Findings come from observed play and targeted code-level diagnostics,
not proof of every map, all canal geometries, long-distance shipping, lock
navigation, powered mills, all difficulty features, or rendered performance.
Temporary forager-first role ordering caused a NoBuilders period and was corrected;
that scripted decision is not counted as a game bug. Summer drying in year two
also followed construction of a dam; the clock defect is independently established
by pre-dam failures and the equal-forcing diagnostic, rather than by assuming all
dry cells had the same cause.
